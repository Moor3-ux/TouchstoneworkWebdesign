Contact and Pre Orders

Use the form below to reserve items for pickup or to ask us a question. We answer requests in the order they come in, usually within one business day. For a custom cake, please give us at least three days of notice, and a week for large sheet cakes.

Allergy and Dietary Notes


Our kitchen works with wheat, eggs, dairy, and tree nuts every day, so we cannot promise an allergen free product. We will tell you honestly what we can and cannot do for your request.
