Welcome to the Neighborhood Bakery

We open the doors at six in the morning, and by then the first bake is already out of the oven. North Star Bakery is a small shop with a short menu, and everything on the shelf was mixed, shaped, and baked here the same day. Stop in for a loaf on your way to work, or call ahead and we will have it waiting.

A Welcome Message From Our Head Baker

What We Are Known For

Bread Baked Twice a Day

Our sourdough and country loaves come out of the oven at six in the morning and again at two in the afternoon, so there is warm bread on the shelf for the evening commute. Anything left at close goes to the food pantry on Third Street.

A Pastry Case Worth the Trip

Croissants, morning buns, cookies, and seasonal tarts fill the case by seven. Commuters tend to clear the shelf early, which is why so many of our regulars put in a pre order the night before.

Cakes for Neighborhood Occasions

Birthdays, retirements, and the occasional Tuesday. We take custom cake requests with at least three days of notice and will work with most allergy needs if you tell us ahead of time.

Featured Item of the Week

This week we are featuring our brown butter oatmeal cookie, sold by the single cookie or by the half dozen. It is a soft cookie with toasted oats and a little sea salt on top, and it is the one our staff keeps sneaking off the tray. Featured items change every Monday.

Want a half dozen set aside? Reserve them the night before and pick them up at the counter.
